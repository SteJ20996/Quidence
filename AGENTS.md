# Product language policy

Stephen approved this policy on 2026-10-24. On 2026-10-29 the repository owner limited quidence.com to English and Spanish; the third rule records that change.

- The Calculus entry is **Think in Motion**, reached through **Test your understanding → Explore the graph & explain** (`modules/ad01/dist/think/`).
- Activities for voice recognition, AI conversation, or direct AI analysis must offer English only, including future subjects. Remove other-language selectors from those activity pages. Set `data-fixed-language="en"` on their HTML root and on links entering them. Future speech/AI services must use English; this policy does not authorize enabling a provider or creating credentials.
- Restrict only these activities to English. Ordinary lessons, foundation checks, problem chains, projects, maps, reports, and their surrounding chooser pages use English and Spanish where available. Do not add other site languages, including Simplified or Traditional Chinese, and do not remove existing English or Spanish translations or language controls. This applies to published site content, not to repository documentation.
- Keep the user's general language preference intact while visiting an English-only activity. Locale URL parameters, reloads, and language changes in another tab must not switch that activity away from English.
- Never rewrite or delete learner answers, saved drafts, or recordings to enforce a language rule. Explain that responses should be in English. Preserve original evidence.
