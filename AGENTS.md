# Product language policy

Stephen approved this policy on 2026-10-24. On 2026-10-29 the repository owner limited quidence.com to English and Spanish. On 2026-11-10 the owner limited Quidence to English only; the rules below record that change.

- The Calculus entry is **Think in Motion**, reached through **Test your understanding → Explore the graph & explain** (`modules/ad01/dist/think/`).
- Quidence is published in English only, including future subjects. Do not add Spanish or any other site language, translation catalogs or language selectors. This applies to published site content, not to repository documentation.
- Activities for voice recognition, AI conversation, or direct AI analysis use English. Keep `data-fixed-language="en"` on their HTML root and on links entering them. Future speech/AI services must use English; this policy does not authorize enabling a provider or creating credentials.
- A `lang` URL parameter or a language preference saved before this change must not switch any page away from English. Leave the saved preference itself in place.
- Never rewrite or delete learner answers, saved drafts, or recordings to enforce a language rule. Explain that responses should be in English. Preserve original evidence, including work written in another language.
