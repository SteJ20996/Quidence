Planned starting points

  1. Output and GDP

    Separate nominal values from real changes; identify what GDP counts and what it omits.

  2. Prices and inflation

    Read an illustrative price index and distinguish a price level from its rate of change.

  3. Employment and unemployment

    Identify the labor force and denominators before interpreting employment measures.

  4. Policy and simplified models

    Explore fiscal and monetary scenarios with stated assumptions and limitations.
