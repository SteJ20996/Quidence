## AA01 · Secondary-school math

範圍：Selected Pre-Algebra and Algebra I foundations。

### aa-f01 · Compare two negative numbers

組別：Numbers and fractions

題幹：Which number is greater: −3/4 or −0.8?

回答欄位：

- Greater number；選項：−3/4 / −0.8 / They are equal

必須解釋：Show how you compared the two values. A decimal conversion or number-line explanation is enough.

參考數學：−3/4 = −0.75, which is to the right of −0.8, so −3/4 is greater.

下一步核實：Compare another negative fraction and decimal before choosing the greater value.

### aa-f02 · Describe a subtraction

組別：Numbers and fractions

題幹：Start at −2 on the number line. Find −2 − 5.

圖/表文字替代：A number line from −8 to 2, with a starting dot at −2.

回答欄位：

- Result；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Explain the direction and distance of your move.

參考數學：Subtracting 5 moves five units left from −2 to −7.

下一步核實：Separate the starting point from the signed movement.

### aa-f03 · Combine parts of one whole

組別：Numbers and fractions

題幹：Find 1/2 + 1/4. You can enter a fraction or a decimal.

回答欄位：

- Sum；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Explain why the pieces need a common size before you add them.

參考數學：One half is two quarters. Two quarters plus one quarter is three quarters, or 0.75.

下一步核實：Use equal-sized parts to add fractions.

### aa-f04 · Find one repeated unit

組別：Rates and proportions

題幹：A runner completes 3 equal laps in 12 minutes at a steady pace. How long do 1 lap and 5 laps take?

回答欄位：

- Minutes for 1 lap；數字，可填分數
- Minutes for 5 laps；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Explain how you found the time per lap and used it for 5 laps.

參考數學：12 ÷ 3 = 4 minutes per lap. Five laps take 5 × 4 = 20 minutes at the same pace.

下一步核實：Find the unit rate, then scale it; keep the units visible.

### aa-f05 · Check a proportional relationship

組別：Rates and proportions

- Is it proportional?；選項：Yes / No

參考數學：The nonzero rows give $3 per bottle, and zero bottles cost $0. The rule is C = 3b.

下一步核實：Check both a constant ratio and the value at zero.

### aa-f06 · Express a part as a percent

組別：Rates and proportions

回答欄位：

下一步核實：Identify the whole before converting a ratio to a percent.

### aa-f07 · Distribute to every term

組別：Expressions and equations

題幹：Choose the expression equal to 2(x + 3) for every x.

回答欄位：

- Equivalent expression；選項：2x + 6 / 2x + 3 / 2x² + 6

必須解釋：Explain what the outside 2 multiplies. You can check your rule at one value of x.

參考數學：The 2 multiplies both x and 3: 2x + 2 × 3 = 2x + 6.

下一步核實：Expand every term inside the parentheses.

### aa-f08 · Keep an equation balanced

組別：Expressions and equations

題幹：Solve 3x − 4 = 11.

回答欄位：

- x；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Show what you do to both sides, and check your value in the original equation.

參考數學：Add 4 to both sides to get 3x = 15, then divide both sides by 3. At x = 5, 15 − 4 = 11.

下一步核實：Explain an equation step as the same operation on both sides.

### aa-f09 · Describe all allowed values

組別：Expressions and equations

題幹：Which inequality describes every solution of 2x + 1 ≤ 9?

回答欄位：

- Solution set；選項：x ≤ 4 / x < 4 / x ≥ 4

必須解釋：Explain the boundary value and whether it is included.

參考數學：Subtract 1 and divide by positive 2 to get x ≤ 4. The boundary x = 4 gives 9, so it is included.

下一步核實：Distinguish a strict inequality from one that includes its boundary.

### aa-f10 · Separate the start from the change

組別：Coordinates and lines

題幹：These points lie on one line. Write its rule as y = mx + b by giving m and b.

給定表：

| x | y |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | -1 |
| 1 | 2 |
| 3 | 8 |

回答欄位：

- Slope m；數字，可填分數
- Starting value b；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Explain which information gives the start and which gives the rate of change.

參考數學：At x = 0, y = −1, so b = −1. From (0, −1) to (1, 2), y increases 3 per unit of x, so m = 3.

下一步核實：Read the starting value and the change separately.

### aa-f11 · Read a signed slope

組別：Coordinates and lines

題幹：A line passes through (−1, 4) and (2, −2). Find its slope.

圖/表文字替代：A decreasing line through the labeled points (−1, 4) and (2, −2).

回答欄位：

- Slope；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Show the change in y and the change in x, including their signs.

參考數學：The slope is (−2 − 4)/(2 − (−1)) = −6/3 = −2.

下一步核實：Use change in y divided by change in x, not a point’s height.

### aa-f12 · Read an ordered pair

### aa-f13 · Separate a fixed fee from a repeated fee

組別：Models and constraints

題幹：A club charges $6 once plus $4 per visit. Choose a rule for total cost C after v visits, then find the cost for 3 visits.

回答欄位：

- Cost rule；選項：C = 6 + 4v / C = 10v / C = 4 + 6v
- Cost for 3 visits ($)；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Explain what each term counts.

參考數學：The $6 is paid once, and 4v counts the visit fees. At v = 3, C = 6 + 12 = $18.

下一步核實：Name the fixed and repeated quantities before building a rule.

### aa-f14 · Check an area expression

組別：Models and constraints

題幹：A rectangle is 3 cm high and w cm wide. Remove a strip 2 cm wide across the full height. For w = 6 cm, find the remaining area.

圖/表文字替代：A 3 cm by 6 cm rectangle. A 2 cm wide strip across the full height is marked for removal.

回答欄位：

- Remaining area (cm²)；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Write an area expression and explain why the removed strip is subtracted.

參考數學：The remaining width is w − 2, so area is 3(w − 2). At w = 6, the area is 3 × 4 = 12 cm².

下一步核實：Distinguish a length removed from an area removed.

### aa-f15 · Check a whole-number limit

組別：Models and constraints

題幹：The club charges $6 once plus $4 per visit. Your budget is $23. Find the largest whole number of visits you can afford, and check that number and one more visit.

回答欄位：

- Largest whole number of visits；數字，可填分數
- Cost for that many visits ($)；數字，可填分數
- Cost for one more visit ($)；數字，可填分數

必須解釋：Explain why your number works and why the next whole number does not.

參考數學：Four visits cost 6 + 4 × 4 = $22. Five cost $26, above the budget. A fractional calculation must be rounded down for whole visits.

下一步核實：Check the proposed limit and the next whole-number case.

## AB01 · Linear algebra foundations

範圍：Vectors, linear combinations, matrix dimensions, and introductory matrix maps。

組別：Vector meaning and operations

- Vertical change；數字，可填分數

參考數學：Subtract the starting point from the endpoint: (2 − (−1), 3 − 1) = (3, 2).

下一步核實：Compare endpoint minus start for arrows in different positions.

### ab-f02 · Add two movements

### ab-f04 · Keep both coefficients

組別：Combinations and reachability

### ab-f05 · Ask what is reachable

組別：Combinations and reachability

題幹：Let u = (1, 2) and v = (2, 4). Can any real coefficients a and b make au + bv = (0, 1)?

回答欄位：

- Can the target be reached?；選項：Yes / No

必須解釋：Describe the line containing every possible combination and compare the target with it.

參考數學：Every combination is (a + 2b, 2a + 4b), so y = 2x. The target (0, 1) is off that line.

下一步核實：A second arrow on the same line does not add an independent direction.

### ab-f06 · Use two independent directions

組別：Combinations and reachability

題幹：Let u = (1, 0) and v = (0, 2). Real coefficients may be positive, negative, or zero. Can au + bv reach every point in the plane?

- Reach every point?；選項：Yes / No

參考數學：au + bv = (a, 2b). For any (x, y), choose a = x and b = y/2.

### ad-f15 · Explain decreasing accumulation
