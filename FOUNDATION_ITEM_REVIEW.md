## AA01 · Secondary-school math

范围：Selected Pre-Algebra and Algebra I foundations。

### aa-f01 · Compare two negative numbers

组别：Numbers and fractions

题干：Which number is greater: −3/4 or −0.8?

回答字段：

- Greater number；选项：−3/4 / −0.8 / They are equal

必须解释：Show how you compared the two values. A decimal conversion or number-line explanation is enough.

参考数学：−3/4 = −0.75, which is to the right of −0.8, so −3/4 is greater.

下一步核实：Compare another negative fraction and decimal before choosing the greater value.

### aa-f04 · Find one repeated unit

### aa-f13 · Separate a fixed fee from a repeated fee

组别：Models and constraints

### aa-f14 · Check an area expression

组别：Models and constraints

题干：A rectangle is 3 cm high and w cm wide. Remove a strip 2 cm wide across the full height. For w = 6 cm, find the remaining area.

回答字段：

- Remaining area (cm²)；数字，可填分数

必须解释：Write an area expression and explain why the removed strip is subtracted.

参考数学：The remaining width is w − 2, so area is 3(w − 2). At w = 6, the area is 3 × 4 = 12 cm².

下一步核实：Distinguish a length removed from an area removed.

### aa-f15 · Check a whole-number limit

组别：Models and constraints

回答字段：

参考数学：Four visits cost 6 + 4 × 4 = $22. Five cost $26, above the budget. A fractional calculation must be rounded down for whole visits.

下一步核实：Check the proposed limit and the next whole-number case.

### ad-f15 · Explain decreasing accumulation
