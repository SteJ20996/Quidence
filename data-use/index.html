Local records and sharing

During public testing, accounts and cloud sync are paused. New learning records stay in this browser when saving is available. Quidence does not upload your answers, drafts, drawings, requests or reports for account sync or model training. Updated October 7, 2026.

Your device keeps your work

Use the same browser profile and site address to resume. Browser clearing, private browsing, a new device or a different site address can make local records unavailable. Download a backup first and confirm it is in Downloads. You can restore that backup locally on another device.

Local records are not protected by a Quidence password. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to read them. Do not enter names, school, contact details or other unnecessary personal information in answers or learning requests.

Delete records before leaving a shared device

The records page can delete Quidence answers, drafts, saved reports, learning requests and older local workspace backups from this browser. Download anything you want to keep first. Close activity and report tabs, remove exported files from Downloads and the trash, and clear clipboard copies. Browser-saved passwords must be removed separately. You can also remove this site’s data in your browser settings.

Clearing this browser does not remove a downloaded copy, material pasted into another service, or records previously uploaded to a server. Existing server records are not fetched or deleted by this local-only release. Automatic record expiry and online account deletion are not enabled.

Sharing with a tutor is your choice

Quidence does not call an AI grading model or automatically send a report to an external tutor. Opening a tutor website does not send it your learning report. Copying creates a clipboard copy; pasting or uploading shares its contents with that service. Review the whole record and remove personal details first. There is no automatic anonymization guarantee.

ChatGPT requires age 13 or the local minimum age, with parent or guardian permission under 18. Claude’s consumer service requires age 18 or the higher local minimum. Provider terms checked October 7, 2026. ChatGPT and Claude are products of OpenAI and Anthropic; Quidence is not affiliated with either provider. Check the providers’ current ChatGPT terms and Claude terms. You can keep learning here or review with a teacher.

What the checks mean

Fixed mathematical components are checked separately. Written explanations need review of their meaning and justification; keyword matches do not confirm understanding. Alternate valid methods, equations and diagrams should be considered. Missing evidence is different from an incorrect mathematical claim. The activities sample selected topics and do not certify mastery, effort, AI use, a complete course diagnosis or learning gains.

This page describes learning-record behavior, not a complete privacy policy. Serving website pages can still involve operational information such as an IP address; the deployed hosting and its logs need separate verification.
