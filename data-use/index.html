How your records are saved

This describes the current software. It is not a complete privacy policy or a promise about an unverified hosting setup. Updated October 6, 2026.

Guest work and account work

Guest answers, unfinished drafts, drawings, report copies and optional learning requests stay in this browser. Anyone using the same browser profile may be able to read them. Browser clearing, a different site address or a different device can make that local work unavailable.

If you sign in, the same kinds of records can be uploaded to the Quidence backend and loaded on another device. Guest work is imported only when you choose to import it. Use a username that does not reveal your full name, school or contact details. Do not enter those details in answers or learning requests.

Accounts store a username, account identifier, password salt and hash, recovery-code hash and creation time. Sessions use an HttpOnly cookie that expires after 30 days. Answers can include what you write, drawings, attempts, support events, timing and saved review notes. The account page lets you download the records currently available on this device; this does not export every older server revision.

Storage and deletion limits

Server record revisions currently have no automatic expiry. The current limit is 50 MiB of revision data per account, with a separate limit per record. A full account does not automatically delete old work; uploads stop and you should download your current work.

Signing out clears account copies and backups from this browser. Guest records remain. “Clear learning records from this device” removes both guest and account learning records here. Neither action deletes the online account or its server history. Downloaded files and browser-saved passwords require separate removal.

Online account deletion and a user interface for older server revisions are not available yet. Server backup, retention and deletion rules still need to be established before a public launch. Hosting providers may process operational logs; the deployed provider and its practices have not been verified here.

Reports and external tutors

Quidence does not call an AI grading model or automatically send your work to an external tutor. Opening an external website does not send it your report. Copying creates a local clipboard copy; pasting or uploading elsewhere shares the contents with that service. Review the whole record first and remove names, school, contact details and other personal information. There is no automatic anonymization guarantee.

ChatGPT requires age 13 or the local minimum age, with parent or guardian permission under 18. Claude’s consumer service requires age 18 or a higher local minimum. These are the providers’ rules, not Quidence’s age policy. Check their current ChatGPT terms and Claude terms. You can continue here or discuss the work with a teacher instead.

What the checks mean

Fixed answers are checked using mathematical rules. Some Algebra explanation suggestions use text patterns and require a review of meaning; other open explanations are retained for review. Alternate valid reasoning should be considered. These activities sample selected topics and do not certify mastery, effort, AI use, a complete course diagnosis or learning gains.
