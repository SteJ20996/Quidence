## AA01 · Secondary-school math

### aa-x01 · Divide portions, not denominators

组别：Fraction operations and signs

题干：You have 3/4 litre of juice. One full cup holds 1/8 litre. How many full cups can you fill?

回答字段与固定答案：

- Full cups；数字，可填分数；答案：6

必须解释：Explain what division asks here. Check your answer by multiplying cups by litres per cup.

参考数学：(3/4) ÷ (1/8) = 6. Six cups use 6 × 1/8 = 3/4 litre. The quotient counts portions of size 1/8, not eighths of 3/4.

下一步核实：Use a new portion size and connect division with how many equal groups fit.

### aa-x02 · Check signs and operation order

组别：Fraction operations and signs

题干：Find −2 × (3 − 5) + 1. A student rewrote it as −2 × 3 − 5 + 1, getting −10. Is that method valid?

回答字段与固定答案：

- Value of the expression；数字，可填分数；答案：5
- Is the described method valid?；选项：Yes / No；答案：No

必须解释：Show the steps, explain the product of two negative numbers, and locate the student’s first invalid step.

参考数学：The parentheses give −2, then (−2)(−2) = 4, then 4 + 1 = 5. The student ignored the parentheses; distributing would require multiplying both 3 and −5 by −2.

下一步核实：Try another negative multiplier and check a correctly distributed expression against the parentheses.

### aa-x06 · When an equation has no unique answer
