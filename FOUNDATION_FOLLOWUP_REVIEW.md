## AA01 · Secondary-school math

### aa-x01 · Divide portions, not denominators

組別：Fraction operations and signs

題幹：You have 3/4 litre of juice. One full cup holds 1/8 litre. How many full cups can you fill?

回答欄位與固定答案：

- Full cups；數字，可填分數；答案：6

必須解釋：Explain what division asks here. Check your answer by multiplying cups by litres per cup.

參考數學：(3/4) ÷ (1/8) = 6. Six cups use 6 × 1/8 = 3/4 litre. The quotient counts portions of size 1/8, not eighths of 3/4.

下一步核實：Use a new portion size and connect division with how many equal groups fit.

### aa-x02 · Check signs and operation order

組別：Fraction operations and signs

題幹：Find −2 × (3 − 5) + 1. A student rewrote it as −2 × 3 − 5 + 1, getting −10. Is that method valid?

回答欄位與固定答案：

- Value of the expression；數字，可填分數；答案：5
- Is the described method valid?；選項：Yes / No；答案：No

參考數學：The parentheses give −2, then (−2)(−2) = 4, then 4 + 1 = 5. The student ignored the parentheses; distributing would require multiplying both 3 and −5 by −2.

下一步核實：Try another negative multiplier and check a correctly distributed expression against the parentheses.

### aa-x04 · Keep track of the percentage base

### aa-x06 · When an equation has no unique answer

組別：Equations and inequalities

題幹：Classify each equation: P is 2(x + 3) = 2x + 6. Q is 2(x + 3) = 2x + 7.

回答欄位與固定答案：

- Equation P；選項：One solution / No solution / Infinitely many solutions；答案：Infinitely many solutions
- Equation Q；選項：One solution / No solution / Infinitely many solutions；答案：No solution

必須解釋：Simplify both sides. Explain what the remaining true or false statement says about all possible x-values.

參考數學：P reduces to 6 = 6 and holds for every real x, so infinitely many solutions. Q reduces to 6 = 7 and holds for no x, so no solution. A cancelled variable does not imply x = 0.

下一步核實：Distinguish an identity, a contradiction, and an equation with a single solution.

必須解釋：Construct the heights and explain the under/over relationship using the increasing function. You may find the exact area geometrically.

參考數學：The width is 1. Left heights 0,1,2 give 3; right heights 1,2,3 give 6. The increasing line makes left rectangles lower and right rectangles higher. The triangular area is 4.5.

### ad-x06 · Net displacement and distance are different

組別：Build a sum with its units

### ad-x08 · Calculate a definite integral and its accumulation slope
